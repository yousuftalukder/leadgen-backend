# Phase 70 — what each kind of work is for; @mentions in task comments

## Run

There's no SQL. Deploy the server on Render, then the pages on Netlify (which happens by itself on merge).

Task emails need mail set up under Team & settings → Email (a Gmail address and its app password), as in phase 68.

## What changed

### Their own numbers, the team's work, tools and outreach are told apart

- **The rail** has two sections:
  - **Clients:** Clients, Websites, Billing, Schedules.
  - **Outreach:** Leads, Pipeline.
- **New work** groups everything by where its numbers come from, with one line on what each kind is for:
  - **Their own numbers · Meta:** the monthly report and the account assistant.
  - **Kept by the team:** the content plan, the ad ledger and stock, message templates, website work.
  - **Tools · public data:** content ideas from public posts, the Instagram audit, Facebook Page report, competitor benchmark, find competitors, group post ideas.
  - **Outreach · find customers:** the lead and group finders, local demand, the review tracker.
- **The order depends on Meta:**
  - **Meta connected:** their own numbers come first, and the public-data tools fold away under a heading. They are still one click away, for content ideas and comparing with rivals.
  - **No Meta yet:** the tools come first, since public data is all there is.
  - **Outreach** is always folded: it is for finding new customers, not for running the account.
- **The tool's content plan is renamed** "Content ideas from public posts", so it isn't confused with the content plan the team types in.
- **A client's tabs** put Meta and the team's work (Content plan, Ad ledger, Message templates) ahead of Reports and Leads.
- **Reports** can be filtered by where the numbers came from:
  - Their Meta;
  - Public data · tools;
  - Built from reports.

  When Meta is connected, the tab says that public-data reports are for content ideas and comparing with rivals.

### @mentions in task comments

- **Mentioning someone:** type `@` in a comment and pick a teammate on the client from the list (arrows and Enter, or click). They show as a highlighted name.
- **Who can be mentioned:** only people on the client. Anything else stays plain text.
- **A mentioned person gets an email at once:** "‹name› mentioned you: ‹task›", with the comment and a link to the task.
- **Business owners:**
  - they can still comment as before, but can't mention;
  - they read the team's mentions as plain "@Name";
  - they are never handed the team list.

### Who hears about a task

- **Who gets the phase-68 "Task updated" email:**
  - whoever holds the task;
  - whoever raised it;
  - (new) everyone on the team who has commented on it.
- **What the email covers:** status, due date, title, priority, description, checklist, comments, files, and the client ticking it off. Several changes inside two minutes go in one email.
- **A mentioned person isn't emailed twice** for the same comment.
- **Unchanged:** a new assignee still hears at once. The person who made the change, business owners and disabled logins never get one.

## Check after deploy

1. Open a client with Meta connected and press New work. Their own numbers and the team's work come first, and Tools and Outreach are folded. On a client without Meta, the tools come first.
2. Open a task and type `@` in a comment. Pick a teammate and send. They get "… mentioned you: …" within a minute.
3. That teammate comments back. The task's assignee and creator get one "Task updated" email about two minutes later.
