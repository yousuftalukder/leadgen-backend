How the Demand Feed works — read this first
The big picture. Edgelead is one app with two halves. The Instagram half (Lead Finder, Performance Audit, Competitor Intel) works on accounts. The Facebook half is these four pages, and they all run on one engine called fb_community — an admin has to grant you that engine and you have to save an Apify token for it, or every Facebook page will just return "access denied". Everything on the Facebook side is about groups only, and public groups only. A private group is skipped on purpose, because reading it would need a logged-in session and this tool will not do that.

The idea

This page is the lead generator, and it is a byproduct. You never "run" the Demand Feed. It fills itself every time discovery or an audit scrapes a room.

Run discovery once, run an audit once, then come back here and the leads are already sitting there. These are people who have publicly said, in the last few days, that they want to buy something. That is a kind of lead that does not exist on Instagram.

How the detection works

While scraping, every single post is run through a buying-intent phrase detector that works in both English and Bangla:

English triggers"looking for", "does anyone know", "can anyone recommend", "need a", "need someone who", "where can I get", "suggest me", "any good", "in search of", "who can help", "best place for", "hiring", "urgently need"
Bangla triggersখুঁজছি / খুজছি / খুঁজতেছি, দরকার / প্রয়োজন / লাগবে, কেউ কি জানেন, কেউ কি আছেন, কোথায় পাবো, সাজেস্ট করুন, রেফার করুন

Most posts produce nothing, and that is correct — a room where every post is a demand signal is a room full of spam. Each post also produces one signal at most, built from its strongest phrase, so the same lead can never be counted twice.

What each lead gets tagged with

Categoryhome services, auto, real estate, food, health, education, tech, beauty, events, legal & finance, logistics, jobs — or "other". Detected in English and Bangla both.
UrgencyHigh for urgent / asap / today / tonight / emergency / জরুরি / এখনই. Medium for "this week", "soon", "by Friday". Otherwise low.
Lead score, out of 100Phrase strength + urgency boost + how specific the post is + how much engagement it already has + how recent it is. Anything 60 or above is counted as hot.

How you use this page

  1. Open it. Leads are already there if you have run discovery or an audit.
  2. Filter by room, category, urgency, or search a keyword like "plumber", "tutor" or "flat".
  3. Read the card — it shows the exact trigger phrase, a snippet of what they asked for, how long ago they posted, and the score.
  4. Click through to the live Facebook post and reply there. That is the whole play. The tool finds the conversation; you have the conversation.
  5. Move the lead through the pipeline as you work it, and add notes.
New→ Saved→ Contacted→ Won or Dropped

Dropped leads are hidden from the default view but never deleted, so a lead you dismissed today will not come back to clutter the feed tomorrow. Export CSV gives you the whole working list to take into a spreadsheet or hand to someone else.

On privacy — this is deliberate. The tool never stores anyone's name. The post author is stored as a one-way hash, purely so it can count unique posters. The link to the post is the identity. You click through and reply on Facebook like a normal person. Nothing here builds a contact database of private individuals.
Speed matters more than volume here. The lead score already fades with age, because a "need a plumber today" post is worthless a week later. Work the high-urgency, high-score rows first and re-run your audits regularly to keep the top of the feed fresh.

Demand feed

Every "anyone know a good…", "looking for…", "need someone who…" pulled out of your audited rooms. This is a lead that does not exist on Instagram. Click through to the live post and reply there — that is the whole play.

Member names are never stored. Each signal keeps a salted one-way hash of the author plus the public post link, which is enough to act on the lead and keeps you clear of storing identifiable personal data without a basis.
