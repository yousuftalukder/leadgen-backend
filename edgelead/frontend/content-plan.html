Content Plan

Built from posts already stored by a Performance Audit or Competitor Intel run — nothing new is scraped, so it costs no Apify credit. Every format × hook × length × topic cell is scored for the target and its rivals. Cells rivals win and the target has never used are gaps; cells the target already wins are double down; cells it posts and loses are stop. Briefs are written only inside those cells, with the evidence posts linked. If the client's Meta account is connected, reach, saves and shares are attached to the target's own posts and shown as insights — never blended with scraped numbers.
