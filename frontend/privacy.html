Privacy

Last updated 22 September 2026

EdgeLead is a social-media analysis and lead-generation tool used by agencies and by the businesses they work with. This page says what it collects, why, where it is kept, and how to have it removed. It is written to be true of what the software actually does.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account. Your email address and, if you give it, your name. Used to sign you in and to show who did what. Held in our database provider (Supabase).
  • Client records you create. Business names, Instagram handles, Facebook Page names, niche and location — the details you type in so reports can be filed under the right business.
  • Public social-media data. When you run a report or a lead search, EdgeLead reads publicly visible information from Instagram and Facebook — profiles, posts, engagement counts, and contact details a business has itself published — through a third-party data provider (Apify). This is used to build the reports and lead lists you asked for.
  • Your Meta data, only if you connect it. If you connect a Facebook Page and its Instagram professional account, EdgeLead reads owner-side insights that Meta makes available to the account holder: reach, views, saves, shares, profile visits, follower demographics, and your own posts' performance. It uses the permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, read_insights, instagram_basic, instagram_manage_insights and business_management. It never posts, messages, edits, or touches ads.
  • Usage inside the product. Which reports were run, when, and roughly what they cost, so allowances and budgets can be enforced.

What we do with it

Build the reports, plans and lists you ask for; write the narrative parts of those reports with an AI model (Google Gemini), which is sent the report's figures and never your credentials; and show your own data back to you and to the people you have chosen to share a client with.

We do not sell personal data. Lead lists you build stay in your account.

Where it lives, and for how long

  • Data is stored with Supabase (database), Render (application server) and Netlify (the pages you see). Access tokens for Meta and API keys for data providers are encrypted at rest.
  • Meta access tokens expire on Meta's schedule (typically 60 days) and are refreshed only by you reconnecting.
  • Reports, lists and client records are kept until you delete them or your account is deleted.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect a Meta account at any time from the Clients page; that deletes the tokens and everything read from Meta for it. If you remove EdgeLead from your Facebook settings, Meta tells us and the same deletion happens automatically — see Delete your data for the details and for checking the status of a request. For anything else, ask your account administrator or contact us below.

Sharing

A report is visible to the account that made it, to people the client record has been shared with inside EdgeLead, and — only if someone creates one — to anyone holding a read-only share link until it expires or is revoked. Nothing is shared with third parties beyond the providers named above, which process it on our behalf.
