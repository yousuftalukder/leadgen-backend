How FB Communities works — read this first
The big picture. Edgelead is one app with two halves. The Instagram half (Lead Finder, Performance Audit, Competitor Intel) works on accounts. The Facebook half is these four pages, and they all run on one engine called fb_community — an admin has to grant you that engine and you have to save an Apify token for it, or every Facebook page will just return "access denied". Everything on the Facebook side is about groups only, and public groups only. A private group is skipped on purpose, because reading it would need a logged-in session and this tool will not do that.

The idea

A group with 80,000 members but no conversation is worthless. A group with 4,000 members where people talk every day is gold. Member count is a vanity number, so this page ignores it and scores the room instead.

Every candidate group gets a real probe scrape — a shallow sample of recent posts — and the ranking is built from what that sample actually shows. Size is only used as a tiebreaker at the very end.

How you use this page

  1. Type a location (say "Rangpur") and a niche ("plumbing"). Or skip both and paste group URLs straight into the seed box.
  2. Press Discover and score rooms. Watch the live log — it names each group as it samples it.
  3. It searches Facebook for matching groups, then pulls roughly 25–40 recent posts from each one to measure how alive it really is.
  4. You get a ranked list with the top 10 flagged. Tick the rooms you want, then either "Save as set" or "Send to audit".

How Room Value is calculated (0–100)

Five signals go into the score. Each one saturates, so a room cannot win on one metric alone.

Liveness — 28%Posts per day. 15 a day or more scores full marks.
Conversation — 30%Median comments per post. 10 or more scores full marks. This is the heaviest weight.
Diversity — 22%How many different people post, versus the same three spammers on repeat.
Demand — 20%What share of posts are people actively asking to buy something. 20% of posts saturates it.
Permission — multiplierThe app reads the group rules. Promotion banned = score cut by 25%. Posts need admin approval = cut another 25%.
Size — tiebreaker onlyAdds a maximum of 5 points, on a log scale. It can break a tie, never win one.

The confidence damper

If the scrape only returned 12 posts, the whole score gets pulled down and the room is flagged provisional. Twelve posts is not enough to call a room, and a thin sample scoring 58 next to a thick sample scoring 28 would be a lie. Anything under 15 posts is labelled "Not enough data — treat as provisional".

Two things you need to know

Facebook's group search is genuinely unreliable. Some runs come back with nothing. That is Facebook, not a bug in this tool. When it happens, paste the group URLs into the Seed group URLs box, or use the Add a room manually tab — those always work, and the rooms get scored exactly the same way.
Save your rooms as a set. Tick the rooms you care about and press "Save as set". A set can be re-run later with one click, and because the same rooms are measured again on the same settings, the Community Audit page can draw you a trend line over time.

What happens next

Discovery tells you which rooms deserve your effort. It does not tell you what to post. For that, send the rooms to Community Audit, which pulls a much deeper sample and works out the winning format, intent, length and posting time for each room.

One thing happens automatically here though: every scrape on this page also mines buying intent out of the posts it reads, so your Demand Feed starts filling with leads the moment discovery finishes. You do not have to run anything to get those.

Find the rooms worth your time

Member count is a vanity number. A 200k group with 4 posts a day is dead; a 6k group with 40 posts a day is where business happens. Every candidate gets a real probe scrape, and Room Value is measured from median comments, unique posters, posting speed and reply latency. Size is only a tiebreaker.
