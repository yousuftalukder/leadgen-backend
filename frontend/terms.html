What EdgeLead is

A tool for analysing social-media accounts, planning content and finding business leads, used by agencies and by the businesses they work with. Reports are built from publicly visible social-media data and, if you connect it, from your own Meta insights.

Your account

  • A self-service account starts with a free trial of a fixed length and fixed allowances, shown to you inside the product. After it ends, access continues only when an administrator activates a plan for you; payment is arranged with the EdgeLead team directly.
  • Employee accounts are created by an administrator and see only the tools and clients they have been given.
  • You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details private and for what is done from your account.

Using it properly

  • Use EdgeLead only for lawful business purposes and in line with Meta's and Instagram's own terms.
  • Contact details in lead lists are ones a business has published itself. Use them respectfully and in line with the marketing and privacy law that applies to you.
  • If you bring your own data-provider API key, its use and cost are yours.
  • Do not attempt to access other accounts' data, overload the service, or resell access.

Your data

What is collected, why, and how to remove it is set out in the privacy page. You can disconnect a Meta account or delete client records at any time.

What we do not promise

Reports are analyses of the data available at the time they were run. Social platforms change what is visible without notice, and third-party data providers can be unavailable. EdgeLead is provided as-is; we do not guarantee any particular business result, and our liability is limited to the extent the law allows.
