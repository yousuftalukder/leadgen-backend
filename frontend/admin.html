Email

Sent from a Gmail account of yours, using an app password (Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords). Business owners need this to sign in to Edge Meta AI: their 6-digit sign-in codes and their invites are sent from it. Until it is set, send each owner a sign-in link yourself (Clients → the owner's row → New sign-in link). It also tells you when someone starts a free trial or asks to continue. Google allows roughly 500 messages a day from one account. The password is stored sealed and is never shown again.

What runs by itself

  • Connect: the owner (in Edge Meta AI, Connect with Facebook) or you (client → Meta → Connect Meta). Reading starts at once, in the background, and takes a few minutes.
  • Every day: at the scheduled times each business is read again: the last days are re-read until Meta settles them, new posts and comments come in, ads follow.
  • Catch-up: after a server restart, anyone whose history was never read is started again, one at a time.
  • Only a person can fix: a login Meta refused or that is about to end (someone reconnects), and a business with no owner login (you invite them).
  • Re-read history reads the 90 days and every post again. Read days re-reads a range you choose (up to 93 days), for a gap marked here.
